Works with PrintStream 1.0.6 or later.

- PrintStream no longer grows Home Assistant's database by gigabytes. The extra details on PrintStream entities (what the cards show, such as temperatures, AMS slots and progress) stay available live but are no longer saved to history; every value that changes over time keeps its history in its own sensor. Home Assistant removes the old entries on its usual schedule (10 days by default); to get the disk space back sooner, run the **Recorder: Purge** action with **Repack** turned on.
